- **Three.js 0.180.0** — MIT, graphics library. License: `node_modules/three/LICENSE`. [Project](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js).
- **ws 8.18.3** — MIT, WebSocket server. License: `node_modules/ws/LICENSE`. [Project](https://github.com/websockets/ws).

Scenery, models and effects in this prototype are generated from local code. No stock art, external fonts, paid media service or external AI API is required to play.
