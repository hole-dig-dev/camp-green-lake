# Camp makeover part 2 — visual references

These photos and film stills informed the shapes and colors of the **original in-game models**. They are reference links, not game textures or redistributed assets.

| Subject | Image source | Design cue used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Water truck | [*Holes* water break still (IMDb)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0311289/mediaviewer/rm1141349377/) | Sun-faded cab, large exposed cylindrical water tank, utilitarian fittings. |
| Guard tower | [Manzanar guard tower photo (Wikimedia Commons)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Manzanar_Guard_Tower.JPG) | Open cross-braced legs and a compact roofed lookout above the fence. |
| Camp landscape | [*Holes* study guide still (ACMI)](https://www.acmi.net.au/education/school-program-and-resources/study-guide-holes/) | Sparse lakebed, canvas shelter palette, harsh sun and simple silhouettes. |

The finished views are in [camp-makeover-part2-screenshots](camp-makeover-part2-screenshots/). The truck, cabins, water tower and watchtowers are still assembled in Three.js so they share the game's flat-shaded look and need no model download.
