# The gold rush: a repeatable gold-mining loop (2026-09-30)

JT and Greg, on a call on 2026-09-30: we're done adding acts. The game becomes a repeatable loop, in the spirit of
gold-mining games like *Hydroneer*: you dig for **volume**, process the sand for gold, and buy things that make you
more gold. Later, the NPC campers do a lot of the work for you (excavators, haulers), and the hazards become things
you buy your way past (pesticide, automatic pesticide dispensers for the lizards).

The Act 1 / Act 2 version is kept on the `archive/acts-era` branch and the tag `acts-era-2026-09-30`.

## What's in (first slice, `feature/gold-rush`)
- **Seeds are gold.** The money is called gold everywhere on screen. In code it's still `S.seeds` (and `sc` on the
  wire), to keep saves and the network format as they were.
- **The bucket.** Shown under Water on the HUD. Every scoop adds sand: a 5 ft hole is one hole of sand
  (`45-state.js fillBucket`). Tier 1 holds 5 holes, the Big bucket 10, the Huge bucket 15 (`SIM.GOLD.buckets`).
  When it's full, your digging still finds things, but the sand goes on the pile.
- **The sifter** (a desert dry washer, `art/blender/sifter.py`, at `SIM.GOLD.SIFTER` between the water truck and the
  Supply Depot). F tips your bucket in. Gold = holes x about 4 (F2 > Gold) x luck: a batch runs from about half to
  double, and about 1 in 16 turns up a nugget (2-4x). `SIM.siftGold`. A dig day doubles it.
- **Mr. Pendanski buys and sells.** The Supply Depot window: a "Sell N finds for X gold" button, and heavy finds sell
  when you carry them to his window (`SIM.SELL` moved there). The gold goes in your own pocket (split between
  everyone carrying a heavy find).
- **The crew bank.** Talk to the Warden to put in 10, 50, half or all of your gold. Everyone sees the total on the
  HUD (where the team quota was). It's for the big shared purchases; there's nothing to spend it on yet.
- **No quota, no getting fired.** Curfew just starts a new day.
- **Water is free**, at the drums by the water truck, as often as you like. The tier 2 canteen (Big canteen) and
  tier 3 (Water jug) hold more, so you come back to camp less.
- **Mr. Sir is gone from camp** (no more selling or water there). His lake walks (the roster "Mr. Sir takes your sack
  if you stand around") are still in.
- Everything else stays: the finds and treasures, the KB tube and the suitcase, the gear, the hazards, the buried
  town, the truck, the north wall.

## The D Tent crew work for the bank (`feature/crew-sifts`)
- Each crew member digs 5 holes, one after another, in fresh ground: past the white line (`CAMP_NODIG` + 1.5 m), out
  of the gate lane (|x| < 4), and nowhere near anyone's hole or another crew member's next spot (`crewDigOK`).
- Their bucket (`art/blender/sifter.py` CampBucket) sits by the hole while they dig, and they carry it by the bail
  when they walk (86-walkie.js `holdProp`, like the first-aid kit).
- Five holes: in through the gate to the sifter. One sifts at a time, the rest wait in a line (`SIFTQ`); they also
  wait (up to 10 s) while a camper's using it. 100% of their gold goes in the crew bank (JT: "for now").
- The crew runs separately on every screen, so only one screen pays: the lowest-id camper's (`crewPaysHere`, like
  the pickup's physics owner). The server only takes crew deposits from the six crew names, up to 600 gold.
