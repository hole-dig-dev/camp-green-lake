Camp Green LakeThere is no lake. Only holes.

Your crew has a 5-day sentence at Camp Green Lake. Every day the Warden wants a quota of sunflower seeds from the crew's shared pile by curfew, or she fires you all. Dig, sell to Mr. Sir, haul heavy finds together, and break into the buried town under the lake. Every mistake feeds the family curse. Survive all five days, then carry Madame Zeroni up Big Thumb before the lake floods.
